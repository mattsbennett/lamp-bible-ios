Strong's numbers and morphology. It is derived from the CrossWire Bible Society KJV module v3.1.

Lamp Bible conversion

Lamp Bible converted the SWORD module into Lamp translation JSON and then into the app's bundled
SQLite database. This changes the container and annotation representation, not the intended
biblical wording. Lamp Bible retains the exact source archive and conversion script in its
private development repository. The original OSIS source is linked above through CrossWire.
